# Document Control System Dashboard
Admin / full DCS home for registrations, inventory lookups, office request attention, and calendar.

> [!NOTE]
> Office Intake users see a different home on `/dcs`. This guide is for **RFIO / Document Controllers** and other full DCS roles.

## Overview chips & stats
- **Total / Latest / Superseded / Obsolete** — open Inventory (Database module) when you have access.
- **Document-type boxes** — Internal Documents, Internal Forms, External Documents, Forms, Logbooks.

## Search Documents
- Search (≥2 characters) opens a **Document Detail** modal with checklist tabs and revision sidebar.
- From the modal you can **Verify checksum** (file tab), **Open in Database**, **Edit document**, or **Stamp** (when permitted).

## Quick Actions (Register module)
- **Register New Document**
- **Register Revised Document**
- **Update Document**

## Needs attention
- Pending office **DRF / DCN** items → **Open Request queue**.

## Today & calendar
- Peek at today’s events; open the calendar panel to browse the month.
- **+ Add Event** and category edits require the **Settings** module.
- **Continue draft** cards resume unfinished registrations.

> [!TIP]
> Saving a registration with **Save and Appraise** continues in RDP Inventory & Appraisal—not on this dashboard.
