# Edit Document Change Notice (DCN)
Correct a DCN when RFIU enabled office editing.

## On this page
- Document finder: Select or confirm the registered source document before saving.
- Form fields: Full CSPC-F-DCC-01 fields prefilled (From / To, Justification of Change, reviewers, approvals).
- Preview form: Review the printable layout.
- Save & resubmit to RFIU: Enabled after the confirm checkbox.

> [!TIP]
> If the wrong source document was selected originally, fix it here before RFIU proceeds to registration.
