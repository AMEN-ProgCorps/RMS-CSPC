# Masterlist
Read-only list of controlled documents your office has already received.

## On this page
- Masterlist: Received copies for your office.
- To receive link: If anything is still pending receive, a header link shows how many to receive and opens Incoming.
- Type filters: All / Internal / Internal Forms / External / Forms / Logbooks appear here only when top tabs are off. With top tabs, use the Documents top-tab bar instead.
- Table columns: Document No., Rev., Title, Effectivity Date, Pages.

## What you cannot do here
- No row actions: No PDF open, edit, mark-as-received, or appraisal buttons on this table.
- Incoming: Receive and Appraisal happen on Incoming.

> [!NOTE]
> Empty state means either nothing received yet, or nothing in the selected type filter.
