# My Document Request Forms (My DRF)
List of DRFs created by your office (form CSPC-F-DCC-06).

## On this page
- New DRF: Start a new document request.
- Recurring DRF: Start a recurring request from a registered document.
- Table columns: Title, Originator, Status, Date, Date Created, Actions.
- Status: Simplified to Registered or Submitted on this list. Richer badges appear on Home under Recent DRF.
- View: Open the saved form.
- Print / Locked: Print when unlocked, or Locked until RFIO notifies print and sign.

## Important rules
- No later edit: Saving a new DRF cannot be edited later unless RFIO unlocks it for correction.
- Hard copy: There is no delete/cancel or submit-online button. Bring the signed hard copy to Records after print.

> [!TIP]
> Full pipeline states (Needs correction, Ready to print, Printed, Awaiting RFIO) are shown on your Home attention and recent cards.
