# Incoming Documents
Distributions sent to your office that are not yet received, plus rows that are received but still awaiting Appraisal.

> [!NOTE]
> RFIO / admin accounts are redirected away from this page. Incoming is per receiving office.

## On this page
- Office header: Names your office and links to Masterlist.
- Table columns: Document No., Rev., Document Title, Effectivity Date, Pages, Action.
- Pagination: Appears at the bottom when there are many rows.

## Actions in the Action column
- Mark as received: Confirms your office took custody.
- Appraisal: Only if your account can access RDP and appraisal is still pending. Opens Inventory and Appraisal with admin values locked; enter Records Location, then create your office record.
- Awaiting Appraisal: Received, but your account cannot open Appraisal (no RDP access).
- No action: Dash means nothing further is required on that row.

## After you receive
- Finish Appraisal: Use Appraisal when the button is available.
- Masterlist: Once appraised, the copy leaves Incoming and appears on Masterlist.

> [!TIP]
> This page has no document PDF viewer, search box, or export. Use Mark as received first, then Appraisal if shown.
