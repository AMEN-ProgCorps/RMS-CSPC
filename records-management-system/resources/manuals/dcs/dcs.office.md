# Office Intake Home
Your office DCS home for Document Request Forms, Change Notices, Incoming copies, and received Masterlist.

> [!NOTE]
> This home is only for Office Intake accounts. RFIO / Document Controllers see a different admin dashboard on the same /dcs URL.

## Quick start
- New DRF: Request a new controlled document (form CSPC-F-DCC-06).
- Recurring DRF: Request another edition of an already registered document.
- New DCN: Request a revision to an existing controlled document (form CSPC-F-DCC-01).

## Stat cards
- My DRF / My DCN: Open your submitted forms.
- Incoming: Distributions not yet received (and items still awaiting Appraisal).
- Masterlist: Documents your office already received.

## Needs your action
- Needs correction: RFIO unlocked a DRF/DCN for edit.
- Ready to print: RFIO notified you to print and sign.
- Needs to be received: Open Incoming and mark as received.
- Needs to be appraised: Finish Appraisal after receive (RDP users).

## Recent lists and guide
- Recent DRF / Recent DCN: Show pipeline badges such as Registered, Needs correction, Ready to print, Printed, Awaiting RFIO.
- How intake works: Create form, print/sign when unlocked, then submit the hard copy to Records / RFIO.

> [!TIP]
> There is no Submit to RFIO button online. After print/sign, bring the signed hard copy to Records.
