# Random Check Results (Office)
View scheduled or upcoming random-check visits and previous results for your office.

> [!NOTE]
> This page is not in the office side nav. Open it from a direct link when provided. Full DCS controllers use the admin Random Check module instead.

## On this page
- Upcoming visits: Cycle/year, visit date, reminder to have distributed copies ready.
- Previous results: Cards with label, date, sample size, and View entire result.

## What you cannot do here
- No admin actions: No schedule creation, draft entry, finalize, or PDF export on the office view.
