# Random Check (Admin)
Schedule and conduct compliance sampling visits, then export finalized PDF reports.

## Screens
1. **Home** — open a year; June/December summary stats
2. **Year** — cycle cards and office grid by cluster (Finalized / Draft / Scheduled); **Schedule Random Check** (office, date, notify)
3. **Office work** — visit date, conducted/tested by; document table (Availability Yes/No, remarks, recommended actions, compliance)
   - Filters: All / With recommended actions / Not complied
   - **Save draft**, **Finalize visit**, **View previous year**, **Export PDF** (after finalized)

> [!NOTE]
> Offices see a separate read-only **Random Check Results** page. Do not share the live admin work screen as the office workflow—send the PDF after finalize.
