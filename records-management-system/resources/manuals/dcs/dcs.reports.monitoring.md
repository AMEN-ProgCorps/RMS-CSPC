# Monitoring Reports
Operational monitoring tables for controlled documents (separate from Generate Report masterlists).

## On this page
- Title: **Monitoring Reports**
- Document-family tabs (Internal, External, …) and **DRF / DCN** tabs
- When a type has children, pick a **sub-type** first or you will see “Select a sub-type”
- Year filter when applicable
- **Filter**, preview table, **Export** (including Excel with column checkboxes)

> [!NOTE]
> Distribution & Retrieval and Syllabi & TOS are separate Monitoring submenu pages.
