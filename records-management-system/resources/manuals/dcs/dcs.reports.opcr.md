# OPCR Targets Report
Generate OPCR target reports and edit rating cells in the preview.

## On this page
- Title: **OPCR Targets**
- OPCR subtype tabs such as Updating of Masterlist, Issuance Internal/External, Controlling Forms/Logbooks/Internal Forms
- Filters + preview table
- Editable **rating Q/E/T/A** and **remarks** cells in the grid
- **Export** CSV (and other formats offered by the toolbar)

> [!NOTE]
> Ratings/remarks edits are part of this OPCR report screen—not the Inventory page.
