# Monitoring of Syllabi & TOS/Rubrics Submission
Track syllabus / TOS-rubrics completion by college program.

## On this page
- Filters: **College**, **Academic Year**, **Semester**, **Course Type**
- Year Level summary is read-only
- Until required filters are set, the page asks you to pick college/year/semester
- Table grouped by Programs with collapsible **SYLLABI**, **DRF**, and **TOS/RUBRICS** columns
- **Remarks** dropdowns save on change

> [!NOTE]
> This monitors submission status—it is not the Syllabi registration wizard on Register Document.
