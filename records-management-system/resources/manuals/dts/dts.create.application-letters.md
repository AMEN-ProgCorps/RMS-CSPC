# Application Letters Manual
Log, route, and track official faculty, staff, and applicant letters such as employment applications, leave endorsements, transfers, and official personnel requests.

> [!NOTE]
> Application letters are routed according to predefined administrative screening sequences or custom workflow routing defined for your college or office.

## Step 1: Applicant & Originator Details
- **Control Number**: System-generated unique tracking sequence for the application.
- **Applicant Name**: Full legal name of the applicant or university personnel.
- **Position Applied / Role**: Target job position, academic rank, or current designation.
- **Originating Office**: Office or department where the application was received or filed.
- **Applying to Unit / College**: The specific university department, college, or administrative office the application is addressed to.

## Step 2: Letter Specifications & Routing Flow
- **Type of Document**: Specific application category (e.g., Application for Employment, Promotion, Leave of Absence, Reclassification).
- **Transaction Flow**: Select standard routing flow (e.g., HRDO Screening, Dean's Endorsement, VPAA Approval, OP Approval).
- **Copy Furnished Offices**: Select secondary offices that should receive carbon copies of endorsements and updates.

> [!TIP]
> Use the **Requestor Quick Dropdown** to auto-fill repeat applicant or personnel profiles without typing names manually.
