# External Transaction Manual
Log and track incoming and outgoing documents exchanged with external agencies, partner institutions, local governments, or outside stakeholders.

> [!NOTE]
> External transactions require recording the external agency's name, their contact person, and receiving dispatch details for audit verification.

## Step 1: External Agency & Originator
- **Agency / Entity Name**: Name of the external government agency, company, NGO, or university partner.
- **Representative / Contact**: Name and designation of the sender or external liaison.
- **Contact Details**: Official email, mailing address, or phone number of the external party.

## Step 2: Document Classification
- **Subject / Description**: Concise summary of the external communication, formal endorsement, or contract.
- **Reference Number**: The external agency's original reference or incoming tracking number (if available).
- **Document Type**: Formal Letter, Memorandum of Agreement (MOA), Audit Query, or Endorsement.

## Step 3: University Assignment & Action
- **Receiving Office**: The CSPC office or college tasked with handling or responding to this external item.
- **Action Required**: Compliance, Endorsement, Review, Legal Assessment, or Filing.

> [!TIP]
> Scan and attach the original signed letter or endorsement to maintain a complete electronic trail alongside the physical document.
