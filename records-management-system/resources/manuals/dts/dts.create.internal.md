# Internal Transaction Manual
> Purpose: For logging and circulating official documents, memos, and requests between CSPC departments, units, and colleges.

## Step 1: Originator Information
- **Control Number**: Auto-generated tracking sequence for the current transaction.
- **Originating Unit/College**: Select your department or college issuing the document.
- **Name of Requestor**: The faculty, administrator, or staff initiating the transaction.
- **Requestor Job Position**: Optional title or role of the requestor (e.g., Dean, Program Head, Faculty).

## Step 2: Document Specifications
- **Subject**: Enter a clear, detailed summary of the document or matter at hand.
- **Type of Document**: Choose the document template or workflow (e.g. Memorandum, Special Order, Travel Order, Letter).

## Step 3: Routing & Action
- **Copy Furnished Offices**: Select all colleges, offices, or departments that need to receive official copies.
- **Action Needed**: Specify the required action (e.g., For Approval, For Signature, For Review, or For Notation).

> [!NOTE]
> Once you click "CREATE TRANSACTION", the system generates a tracking barcode slip and initiates the routing flow.

> [!TIP]
> Ensure all physical documents are stamped and signed before sending them with the printed barcode tracking slip.
