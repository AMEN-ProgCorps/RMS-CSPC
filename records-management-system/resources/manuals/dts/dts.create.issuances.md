# University Issuances Manual
Create, disseminate, and track institutional policies, administrative orders, university memoranda, office orders, and board circulars across CSPC offices.

> [!NOTE]
> Issuances can be broadcast university-wide using the **[HUB]** or **ALL Offices** option, ensuring all departments receive notification simultaneously.

## Step 1: Issuance Categorization
- **Issuance Type**: Select between University Memorandum (NM), Office Order (OO), Special Order (SO), or Executive Directive.
- **Sequence / Series Number**: Official issuance tracking number following the office's annual numbering sequence.
- **Subject**: Comprehensive title and summary of the directive, policy, or announcement.

## Step 2: Distribution & Routing
- **Receiving Offices**: Specify individual recipient offices or select **ALL** to distribute across all university colleges and units.
- **Transaction Flow**: Choose linear multi-step approval or direct free-flow issuance dissemination.
- **Copy Furnished**: Include archival repositories, legal offices, or administrative committees as CC recipients.

> [!TIP]
> When broadcasting an issuance to multiple departments, check **Hub Distribution** to enable parallel acknowledgments from all recipient units.
