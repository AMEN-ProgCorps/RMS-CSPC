# Forwarded Documents
Review all documents that have been processed and released from your office to subsequent departments.

> [!NOTE]
> Once a document is forwarded, custody transfers to the destination office. You can track their acknowledgment and progress from here.

## Tracking & Logs
- **Destination Office**: Shows the target department assigned to take the next action.
- **Forwarded Timestamp**: Exact date and time the document was released by your staff.
- **Receipt Status**: Displays whether the destination unit has physically accepted the incoming item.

> [!TIP]
> If a document was forwarded to the wrong office by mistake, you can recall it before the destination office confirms receipt.
