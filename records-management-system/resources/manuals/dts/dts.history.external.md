# External Transactions History & Archive
Historical audit ledger of all closed, complied, and finalized transactions with external government and private institutions.

> [!NOTE]
> Physical courier airway bills, external delivery slips, and final acknowledgment letters are indexed here for COA audit compliance.

## Audit Search
- **External Agency**: Look up past transactions involving specific government departments, LGUs, or vendors.
- **Reference Code**: Search by historical external reference number or control sequence.
- **Date Completed**: Filter transactions concluded within specific audit periods.

## Archival Data
- **Delivery Confirmation**: Recorded recipient signature and dispatch timestamp.
- **Archival Package**: Access scanned transmittal manifests and official responses.
