# Internal Transactions History & Audit Archive
Historical archive of completed, archived, and finalized internal university transactions.

> [!NOTE]
> Completed records are retained in accordance with university document retention schedules and cannot be modified or altered.

## Filter & Export Tools
- **Fiscal / Academic Year**: Filter completed records by academic semester or calendar year.
- **Search by Origin / Destination**: Audit movement routes across colleges and administrative divisions.
- **Export Data**: Generate and download printable audit manifests or spreadsheet reports.

## Information Displayed
- **Full Routing Log**: Permanent timestamped ledger of every office that processed the document.
- **Final Action & Resolution**: Recorded outcome, signature dates, and archival notes.
- **Digital Attachment Archive**: Access archived PDF scans and supporting document files.
