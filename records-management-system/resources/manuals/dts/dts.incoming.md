# Incoming Transactions
All pending documents currently en route or awaiting receipt and verification by your office personnel.

> [!NOTE]
> Receive documents promptly upon physical arrival to timestamp legal custody and update the university-wide audit trail.

## Action & Verification
- **Receive Button**: Marks physical custody as accepted by your office and updates the tracking timeline.
- **Preview / View Details**: Inspect document subjects, attached files, and endorsements prior to accepting.
- **Batch Receive**: Select multiple incoming items and accept custody in a single click.

> [!TIP]
> You can also use the **Barcode Scanner Console** to receive documents instantly with a barcode gun.
