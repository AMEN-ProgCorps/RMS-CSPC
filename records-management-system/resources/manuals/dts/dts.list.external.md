# List of External Transactions
Monitor and administer all incoming endorsements, audit communications, agency requests, and outgoing dispatches involving external entities.

> [!NOTE]
> Physical couriers, receipt confirmations, and external reference stamps should be cross-checked before releasing or archiving external records.

## Search & Filters
- **Agency Search**: Filter transactions by external agency name, partner university, LGU, or contractor.
- **Reference Code**: Search by the external entity's original communication or tracking reference number.
- **Status Filter**: View In-Transit, Received, For Action, Complied, or Dispatched transactions.

## Table Columns & Actions
- **Control Tracking Code**: Click to view the full routing history and audit trail in the **Transaction Details Modal**.
- **External Sender**: Displays the outside institution and liaison representative.
- **Assigned University Office**: Shows the internal CSPC unit responsible for processing or compliance.
- **Row Actions**: Quickly Receive, Forward, or Print barcode slips.
