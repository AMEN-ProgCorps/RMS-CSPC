# Document Tracking System Dashboard
The central operations portal for tracking, receiving, processing, and monitoring all document flows routed through your office.

> [!NOTE]
> Incoming documents appear automatically in real-time as soon as the previous office forwards them.

## Workflow Tabs
- **Incoming Tab**: All pending documents currently en route or awaiting receipt by your office personnel.
- **My Transactions**: Documents originally created or filed by members of your office.
- **Received**: Active documents physically received and currently undergoing evaluation or action in your office.
- **Forwarded**: Documents your office has completed and released to subsequent recipient units.

## Layout & View Options
- **Table View**: Compact data-dense table with sorting, search, and batch operation checkboxes.
- **Card / Box View**: Grid view displaying visual workflow badges and summary chips.
- **Group by Office**: Group incoming records by their sender office for prioritized batch processing.

> [!TIP]
> Click any row or control number to open the **Transaction Details Modal** and see the complete timeline history.
