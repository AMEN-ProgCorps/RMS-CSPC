# My Transactions
Track all communications, memoranda, and requests initiated by your department or college.

> [!NOTE]
> Review this section regularly to check which offices have custody of your documents and track processing turnaround time.

## Status Indicators
- **In-Transit**: The document was released by your office and is currently moving to the next receiving office.
- **Received**: The destination office has confirmed physical receipt and is processing the request.
- **Returned for Revision**: The document requires amendments or missing attachments before proceeding.
- **Completed**: The document workflow has completed all routing steps.

## Actions Available
- **Print Slip**: Re-print barcode routing slips if the physical copy is damaged or lost.
- **Cancel / Revoke**: Cancel pending transactions before receiving offices accept custody.
