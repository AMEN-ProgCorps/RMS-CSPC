# Quick Receive Console
Dedicated intake interface for rapid physical document acceptance, barcode validation, and custody receipt logging.

> [!NOTE]
> Physical custody transfers legally to your unit the moment a document is confirmed received.

## Intake Process
- **Enter Tracking Barcode**: Scan or manually enter the transaction sequence number from the document slip.
- **Verify Subject & Origin**: Confirm that the physical document in hand matches the screen description.
- **Confirm Receipt**: Press Enter or click **Receive** to stamp arrival timestamp into the system.

> [!TIP]
> If a barcode is smudged or unreadable, type the printed control number directly into the search field.
