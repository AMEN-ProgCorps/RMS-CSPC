# Received Documents
Manage all documents currently in the physical custody and active processing queue of your office.

> [!NOTE]
> Documents here remain under your office's responsibility until you officially forward them to the next office or complete the transaction.

## Workflow Actions
- **Forward Transaction**: Route the document to the next scheduled department or select specific offices.
- **Add Notes / Remarks**: Record action taken, review comments, or recommendations into the permanent audit history.
- **Mark as Completed**: Finish the transaction lifecycle if your office is the final destination.
- **Return for Revision**: Return the document back to the originator or previous handler with compliance notes.

> [!TIP]
> Ensure all official review stamps, signatures, and physical notations are completed on the physical document before clicking **Forward**.
