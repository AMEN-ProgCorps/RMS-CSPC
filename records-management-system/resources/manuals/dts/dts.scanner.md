# Advanced Scanner Console
High-speed document intake console supporting physical barcode scanner guns, camera-based barcode/QR scanning, continuous batch processing, and instant workflow routing.

> [!NOTE]
> Ensure the cursor is focused inside the scan input field or enable **Auto-Action** mode for hands-free barcode gun scanning.

## Scanning Modes
- **Hardware Barcode Gun**: Plug-and-play USB or Bluetooth laser scanner support. Barcodes are instantly decoded upon scan.
- **Webcam / Camera Scanner**: Activate the device camera to scan QR codes and 1D barcodes directly on screen.
- **Continuous Mode**: Keep the scanner listening for back-to-back documents without requiring manual confirmation between items.

## Instant Workflow Actions
- **Auto-Receive**: Automatically accept incoming documents upon barcode scan without clicking confirmation.
- **Auto-Forward**: Move received documents directly to the next scheduled recipient unit.
- **Return for Revision**: Flag documents with missing attachments or incorrect endorsements.
- **Complete Transaction**: Mark documents as officially finalized upon arrival at the final archival destination.

> [!TIP]
> Use Continuous Mode when receiving morning courier deliveries or batch inter-office memorandum distributions.
