# Internal Transactions
Monitor, track, forward, receive, and inspect all internal documents routed through your office.

> [!NOTE]
> Transactions marked in bold or with pending badges require immediate review or forward action by your office personnel.

## Filter & Search Tools
- **Status Filter**: Toggle between Pending, In-Transit, Received, Completed, and Archived records.
- **Search Bar**: Search by Tracking Code, Document Subject, Originating Unit, or Requestor Name.
- **Date Range**: Filter transactions issued or received within specific academic or calendar terms.

## Table Columns & Actions
- **Tracking Code**: Click any tracking number to open the **Transaction Details Modal** with complete audit history and timeline.
- **Document Subject**: Displays the title and category of the communication.
- **Current Office**: Highlights which office currently holds physical custody of the document.
- **Action Buttons**: Quickly Receive, Forward, Release, or Print barcode slips directly from the row actions.

> [!TIP]
> Use the multi-select checkboxes to perform batch actions such as receiving or forwarding multiple incoming files simultaneously.
