# DRF Preview
Printable preview of Document Request Form CSPC-F-DCC-06 before or after save.

## What you see
- Form layout with originator, title, type, distribution, and signatories.
- Preview opens in a new tab from the Preview form button.

## Tips
- Use Preview to verify layout before Save (cannot edit later).
- Enter in form fields does not open preview — click the button.

> [!NOTE]
> Preview does not submit the DRF. Confirm the checkbox, then Save.
