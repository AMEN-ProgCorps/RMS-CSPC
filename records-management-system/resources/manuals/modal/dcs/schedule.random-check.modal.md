# Schedule Random Check Modal
Admin Random Check tool for planning an office visit.

## Fields
- Pick the office to visit
- Visit date
- Notify option when offered by the form

## After scheduling
- The office appears on the year grid as Scheduled / Draft / Finalized
- Open the office work screen later to record availability, remarks, recommended actions, and compliance
- Finalize the visit before exporting the PDF report

> [!NOTE]
> Office users do not schedule visits here. They only view Random Check Results for their office.
