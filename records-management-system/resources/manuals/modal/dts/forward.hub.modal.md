# Forward HUB Selection Modal
Select multiple university offices to simultaneously receive copies or parallel action requests for this document.

> [!NOTE]
> In Hub distribution mode, each selected office receives its own independent acknowledgment queue.

## Office Selection
- **Search Office**: Search across all colleges, academic programs, and administrative units.
- **Select All**: Quickly check all university offices for campus-wide memorandum dissemination.
- **Selected Offices List**: Review and remove specific units from the dispatch manifest.

> [!TIP]
> Ensure only relevant offices are selected to prevent unnecessary incoming queue clutter for unrelated units.
