# Forward Transaction Modal
Route documents to the next scheduled university office, specify actions required, and attach remarks or compliance notes.

> [!NOTE]
> Once you click **Forward**, custody is transferred to the receiving office. They will be notified immediately on their Incoming dashboard.

## Forwarding Configuration
- **Forward to Office**: The target department or unit scheduled to receive the physical file.
- **Action Needed**: Specify the required action (For Action, For Approval, For Signature, For Review, or For Notation).
- **Remarks / Notes**: Optional comments, instructions, or compliance items for the receiving officer.
- **Urgency / Priority**: Mark as Normal, Urgent, or Rush processing.

> [!TIP]
> If routing to multiple offices simultaneously, use the **Forward HUB Modal** to select multiple recipient departments.
