# Receive Transaction Confirmation Modal
Confirm legal acceptance and physical custody of an incoming document into your office.

> [!NOTE]
> Receiving stamps your office name, receiving officer account, and exact timestamp into the university tracking ledger.

## Verification Checklist
- **Tracking Code**: Verify barcode or control sequence matches the physical slip.
- **Physical Documents**: Ensure all attached physical pages, vouchers, and attachments are present.
- **Receiver Notes**: Add intake remarks if physical copies have notes or initial condition issues.

> [!TIP]
> After confirming receipt, the document will move from your **Incoming** tab into your **Received** queue.
