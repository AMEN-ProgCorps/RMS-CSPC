# Transaction Details Modal
View the complete lifecycle history, routing path, current custody, and attached digital files for this transaction.

> [!NOTE]
> All movements, receptions, forwards, and status updates are logged in real time with the handler's office name and timestamp for accountability.

## Header & Overview
- **Control Number**: Unique university tracking identifier assigned upon initial creation.
- **Document Title / Subject**: The recorded summary of the transaction's purpose and contents.
- **Originating Unit**: The department, college, or external entity that initiated the document.
- **Current Status**: Displays whether the document is Pending, In Transit, Received, or Completed.

## Routing Timeline & History
- **Movement Steps**: Sequential step-by-step audit log showing each office that handled the document.
- **Timestamps**: Exact date and time the document was released, received, or forwarded.
- **Remarks & Notes**: Comments, endorsements, or instructions attached by each reviewing officer.

## Attachments & Quick Actions
- **File Previews**: Click any uploaded PDF or scanned document thumbnail to view the document.
- **Print Barcode Slip**: Re-print the physical tracking routing slip if needed.
- **Forward / Receive**: If the document is currently assigned to your office, perform forward or receive actions directly here.

> [!TIP]
> Check the Remarks column to see specific instructions or compliance requirements left by previous receiving offices.
