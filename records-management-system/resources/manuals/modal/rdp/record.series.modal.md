# Record Series Selection & Hierarchy Modal
Select an approved National Archives record series classification or build custom sub-series structures for inventory encoding.

> [!NOTE]
> Selecting a standard General Records Schedule (GRS) series automatically pre-populates official NAP disposition retention rules and legal provisions.

## Hierarchy Navigation & Selection
- **Series Category**: Filter records series by standard functional categories (Administrative, Financial, Personnel, Academic).
- **Parent Series**: The overarching classification grouping (e.g., "Correspondence", "Personnel Folders - 201 Files").
- **Sub-Series / Specific Type**: Secondary breakdown or specific record form type under the parent series.
- **Custom Series**: If the record item is unique to your college/unit and not covered by national schedules, encode a custom office series.

## Search & Filters
- **Search Query**: Type keywords or NAP series code numbers into the search field to filter items instantly.
- **Office Scope**: View university-wide standard series or records series specific to your assigned college.

> [!TIP]
> Use the search box at the top of the modal to instantly locate record series by keyword or NAP series number.
