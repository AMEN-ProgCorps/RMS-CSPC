# Records Inventory and Appraisal (NAP Form 1)
Encode and appraise office records in accordance with the National Archives of the Philippines (NAP) standards to establish official disposition retention periods.

> [!NOTE]
> All submitted records undergo appraisal evaluation before being compiled into the official Records Disposition Schedule (NAP Form 2) and requesting disposal authority (NAP Form 3).

## Record Series Selection
- **Record Series Title**: Select an official NAP series or enter a customized series name representing the group of related records.
- **Series Type / Classification**: Choose whether the series falls under General Records Schedule (GRS) or Agency-Specific Records Schedule (RDS).
- **Subsections / Sub-series**: Specify child record series or specific form titles if applicable.

## Record Description & Volume
- **Period / Years Covered**: The inclusive date range from earliest to latest record in the series (e.g., 2020 - 2023).
- **Description**: Detailed description of the documents included, specific forms, purpose, and content.
- **Volume**: Estimated bulk of records quantified by folder count, box count, linear meters, or megabytes/gigabytes for digital files.
- **Records Medium**: Medium format (Paper, Electronic / Digital, Microform, Audio-Visual).
- **Restriction**: Classification of access (Open Access, Restricted, Confidential, or Privileged).

## Retention Period & Appraisal
- **Time Value**: Archival value designation (Permanent / Historical vs Temporary / Disposable).
- **Utility Values**: Administrative, Fiscal, Legal, or Research utility reasons for retaining the records.
- **Active & Storage Period**: Number of years maintained in the active office space and in secondary storage/warehouse before disposition.
- **Disposition Provision**: The planned final action upon retention expiration (Disposal / Destruction, Permanent Transfer to NAP Archive, or Indefinite Retention).

> [!TIP]
> You can save progress at any time using **Save as Draft** and resume later under the **Draft > Inventory and Appraisal** menu without losing entered data.
