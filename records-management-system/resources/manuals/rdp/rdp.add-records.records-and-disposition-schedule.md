# Records and Disposition Schedule Setup (NAP Form 2)
Establish and configure university-wide Records Disposition Schedules defining retention periods, active office years, storage years, and legal provisions for each record series.

> [!NOTE]
> Schedules established here serve as official university policy submitted to the National Archives of the Philippines (NAP) for formal approval and validation.

## Series Configuration
- **Item Number**: Sequential series index on the official disposition schedule.
- **Series Title & Description**: Standard title of the records classification and description of covered documents.
- **Parent / Sub-Series Hierarchy**: Establish parent-child groupings for specific sub-categories or departmental forms.

## Retention Periods & Legal Provisions
- **Active Period**: Duration (in years) the records must remain in active office space for day-to-day operations.
- **Storage Period**: Duration (in years) the records are stored in secondary storage, archive warehouse, or records center.
- **Total Period**: Cumulative retention span before disposal eligibility.
- **Permanent Flag**: Check if the record holds historical, legal, or archival value requiring permanent preservation.
- **Remarks & Provisions**: Statutory references, COA circulars, or NAP guidelines governing retention.
