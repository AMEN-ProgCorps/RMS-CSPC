# Draft Inventory and Appraisal Records
Review, resume editing, and submit unfinalized NAP Form 1 appraisal records saved by your office.

> [!NOTE]
> Records saved as draft remain private to your office and will not be forwarded for administrative appraisal until you click Submit.

## Draft Management
- **Resume Encoding**: Click any draft row to reopen the full appraisal form with all previously saved data pre-populated.
- **Batch Actions**: Select multiple draft items to submit simultaneously or delete obsolete entries.
- **Last Modified Timestamp**: Review who last edited the record draft and when.
