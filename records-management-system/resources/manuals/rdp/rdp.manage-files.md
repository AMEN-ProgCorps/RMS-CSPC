# Manage Files
Central repository to browse, inspect, organize, and administer archived records, physical location coordinates, and attached digital documents.

> [!NOTE]
> Physical locations and electronic attachments linked to completed transactions transferred from DTS and DCS can be retrieved and inspected here.

## File Search & Filtering
- **Office Filter**: Narrow down records originating from or held by a specific university college or administrative unit.
- **Search Query**: Look up records by document code, barcode, subject title, or series keyword.
- **Record Series Tag**: Filter by National Archives standard classification grouping.

## Document Operations
- **View Attachment**: Preview PDF scans and supporting evidence files directly in the browser viewer.
- **Physical Coordinates**: Inspect the assigned building, room, filing cabinet, shelf, or warehouse box index.
- **Export File Manifest**: Generate printable inventories of archived records for annual audit compliance.
