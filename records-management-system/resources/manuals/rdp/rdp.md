# Records Disposition Program Dashboard
Executive dashboard for monitoring university-wide records inventory, active appraisal schedules, incoming document transfers, and disposal requests.

> [!NOTE]
> Transferred retention records from DTS and DCS are synchronized automatically with the central records disposition database.

## Dashboard Overview & Queues
- **Transferred Documents Tab**: Quick view of completed transactions received from DTS, DCS, and external Record Requests awaiting appraisal.
- **Cluster Filter**: Filter appraised record clusters by NAP Form 1, Form 2, or Form 3 status.
- **Appraisal Status Cards**: Visual indicators of records categorized under Permanent vs Temporary retention periods.

## Quick Actions
- **Add New Series**: Open quick modal to define an agency-specific record classification.
- **Inspect Cluster**: Preview grouped record inventories scheduled for administrative verification.

> [!TIP]
> Use the search bar to locate specific record series or transaction tracking numbers across all disposition queues.
