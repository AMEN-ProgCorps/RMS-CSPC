# Records Pending for Approval
Review, evaluate, and formally approve or return records inventory appraisal submissions from university departments.

> [!NOTE]
> This console is restricted to Records Officers and authorized disposition committee administrators.

## Appraisal Evaluation
- **Verify Classification**: Cross-examine proposed record series against official NAP General Records Schedules.
- **Audit Retention Periods**: Confirm that active and storage years comply with national archives regulations.
- **Approval Actions**: Approve for inclusion into official NAP Form 2 or return with compliance findings.
