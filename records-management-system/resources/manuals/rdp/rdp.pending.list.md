# Pending Records Inventory Submissions
Track the evaluation, review status, and verification progress of records inventory appraisal submissions awaiting approval.

> [!NOTE]
> Submitted records are locked from further editing while under administrative and committee appraisal review.

## Review Stages & Tracking
- **Submission Date**: Timestamp when the inventory was finalized and forwarded by your office.
- **Assigned Evaluator**: Records Management Officer or committee member tasked with appraisal review.
- **Current Status**: Awaiting Appraisal, Under Evaluation, Needs Clarification, or Approved.
