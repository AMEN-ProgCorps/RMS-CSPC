# Received Documents from DCS
Intake and appraise formal documents, approved templates, and controlled records transferred from the Document Control System (DCS).

> [!NOTE]
> Controlled forms and ISO quality procedures transferred from DCS require mapping to National Archives series before establishing storage timelines.

## Form & Template Metadata
- **Form Code / Series**: Official ISO control sequence and revision number from DCS.
- **Title / Procedure**: Document procedure title and responsible committee.
- **Date Approved**: Date when the document was finalized and designated for archival appraisal.

## Archival Actions
- **Appraise & Classify**: Map the controlled document to general records schedule or agency retention rules.
- **Archive File**: Move directly into permanent university reference storage.
