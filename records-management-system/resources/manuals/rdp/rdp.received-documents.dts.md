# Received Documents from DTS
Review and intake completed transactions transferred from the Document Tracking System (DTS) into the Records Disposition Program.

> [!NOTE]
> Only fully-completed DTS transactions with complete routing audit trails may be imported into RDP for formal inventory and appraisal.

## Intake Process
- **Transaction Code**: Unique control sequence assigned during initial DTS logging.
- **Subject & Description**: The original transaction matter and summary notes.
- **Transferred Date**: Exact timestamp when the document completed routing and was transferred to RDP.

## Available Actions
- **Appraise Record**: Initiate NAP Form 1 appraisal evaluation to classify the record series and set retention periods.
- **View Routing Trail**: Inspect historical handling officers, remarks, and attachments from DTS.
