# Received Record Requests
Manage, verify, and fulfill public Freedom of Information (FOI) and internal document reproduction requests received from the online portal.

> [!NOTE]
> Review data privacy classifications and restriction levels before releasing copies of archived records.

## Request Details
- **Requestor Information**: Name, affiliated institution, and contact details of the applicant.
- **Requested Documents**: Specific transcript, appointment order, board resolution, or financial document requested.
- **Purpose**: Academic, employment, legal, or personal verification reason.

## Action & Status
- **Process Request**: Locate the physical or digital document in Manage Files and fulfill the request.
- **Status Log**: Record release timestamp, certified true copy issuance, or denial with justification.
