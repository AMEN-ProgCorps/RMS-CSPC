# National Archives References & Series Catalog
Browse standard record classification schedules, approved NAP retention periods, and statutory disposal authorities.

> [!NOTE]
> References are categorized into General Records Schedules (GRS - common across government agencies) and Agency Specific Records Schedules (RDS - tailored to higher education).

## Reference Navigation
- **Filter by Type**: Toggle between GRS (Administrative, Financial, Personnel, Legal) and university-specific academic schedules.
- **Search Series**: Look up record titles, retention years, and disposal instructions by keywords.
- **Legal Authority**: View official Republic Acts, NAP circulars, and executive orders governing each record series.
