# Master Records Lifecycle Report
Comprehensive university-wide ledger visualizing the complete lifecycle status of all physical and electronic documents across departments.

> [!NOTE]
> The lifecycle tracks documents through four primary stages: Creation/Active Use, Secondary Storage, Permanent Retention, or Disposal Authorization.

## Lifecycle Metrics & Analytics
- **Stage Distribution**: Visual breakdown of records currently Active, in Storage, or Eligible for Disposal.
- **Office Compliance**: Monitor departmental submission rates and appraisal timeliness.
- **Archival Ingestion Rate**: Track digital file bundles and storage consumption over academic years.
