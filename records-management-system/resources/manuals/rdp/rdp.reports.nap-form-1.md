# NAP Form 1 Report (Records Inventory and Appraisal)
Generate, cluster, and export the official National Archives of the Philippines Form 1 report documenting physical and digital records across offices.

> [!NOTE]
> NAP Form 1 serves as the primary baseline document required before any agency disposal or retention schedule can be validated.

## Clustering & Selection
- **Select Records**: Check inventory entries to combine into an appraisal cluster batch.
- **Create Cluster**: Name and save clusters for departmental submission or board endorsement.
- **Filter by Office & Retention**: Narrow down by academic unit, permanent status, or temporary disposal eligibility.

## Export & Print
- **Official NAP Layout**: Generates formatted letter-sized forms adhering to National Archives layout guidelines.
- **Include Descriptions**: Option to include detailed document abstracts or concise series headers on print.
