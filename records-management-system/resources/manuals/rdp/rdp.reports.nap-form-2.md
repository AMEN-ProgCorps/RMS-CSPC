# NAP Form 2 Report (Records Disposition Schedule)
Generate and export the formal university Records Disposition Schedule matrix detailing statutory retention periods and disposal provisions.

> [!NOTE]
> Form 2 requires formal board approval and submission to the National Archives of the Philippines (NAP) for executive accreditation.

## Schedule Matrix
- **Item Series Number**: Official indexing code for each record group.
- **Record Series Title & Description**: Scope of records covered under the schedule item.
- **Retention Periods**: Breakdown of active office years, storage years, and total retention duration.
- **Disposal Provisions**: Planned action upon expiration (e.g., Shredding, Archival Transfer, Permanent Retention).
