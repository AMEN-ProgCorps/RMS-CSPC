# NAP Form 3 Report (Request for Authority to Dispose of Records)
Compile eligible expired records and generate the official application for disposal authority submitted to the National Archives of the Philippines.

> [!NOTE]
> Physical destruction of government records without an approved NAP Form 3 and presence of an official NAP witness constitutes a legal violation under RA 9470.

## Disposal Application Details
- **Eligible Expired Records**: Records whose total retention period (active + storage) has officially elapsed.
- **Volume & Weight**: Estimated bulk (cubic meters, box count, or kilograms) intended for destruction.
- **Mode of Disposal**: Proposed destruction method (e.g., Shredding, Pulping, Recycling, or Incineration).
- **COA Clearance**: Verification that no pending audit queries or court proceedings affect the listed records.
